# The vqopb Era: Qulicker & Xiroach

Following the early era and the NO CORPSES IN OUR KITCHEN period, the album vqopb (recorded in February, released July 2024) represents a unique turning point in Gerasenko's discography, released under the joint project Qulicker & Xiroach. In terms of sonic evolution, this release is a vast, boundary-pushing audio thriller. It combines the cinematic minimalism and intimate atmosphere of his "kitchen" diptych with an unexpected return to complex, broken club structures of the 1990s and 2000s.

On this album, Gerasenko and Xiroach perform a bold manoeuvre: they take the melancholic, isolated atmosphere as their foundation and cross it with a multitude of genres, from hard techno and acid to drum and bass and future garage.

Analysing this expansive *18-track* release, four key musical blocks can be identified:

1.    *Cinematic sound design and gaming atmosphere. * "we are here" (00:46) — a short intro reminiscent of the opening of a disaster film or a field recording from an empty space. "arsenal gear ambient" (05:08) — a direct, deep reference to the cult Metal Gear Solid universe, a cold, spy-themed, paranoid ambient track perfectly capturing the feeling of being inside a vast, flooded technological complex.

2.    *Psychedelic acid and harsh IDM. * *"f12+acid 7 ebs" (09:15)* — the longest and most monumental track on the release, crossing endless acid modulations with a complex, exhausting IDM rhythm. "2st vqopb bs" (05:32) and "vqopb" (06:57) — the central title themes, where broken digital glitches and a dry, uncompromising rhythmic framework collide with dirty lo-fi texture.

3.    *Breakbeat, drum and bass, and rhythmic chaos. * "cactusi dinow" (07:04) and "from lincoln to surgut" (08:36) — tracks demonstrating Gerasenko's mastery of fast, broken beats, crossing old-school drum and bass, jungle, and overloaded techno with digital errors and harsh "Siberian" flavour. *"flts+16 dstrloop [ml mix]" (08:03)* — a hard techno track built on destructive distortion loops, aggressive, noisy, and dirty.

4.    *Weirdcore paranoia. * "window peeper" (05:48) and *"whatthefuck?+withsounds7" (06:32)* — compositions that sound like direct heirs to the "schizophrenic" period, using everyday noises and strange sound artefacts to create pure paranoid discomfort.

The album vqopb is an incredibly dense and diverse collage. The artists proved that the ability to work with pure, deep ambient can be successfully integrated into dynamic breakbeat and techno structures, creating a captivating electronic tapestry.

Why vqopb Stands Alongside the Kitchen Diptych?

If I AM ABSOLUTELY SURE THERE ARE NO CORPSES IN OUR KITCHEN represented an internal, psychological revolution, then vqopb became a technical revolution and a massive step forward. This album deserves equal importance because it documented a crucial transition: Gerasenko learned to take the fragile, cinematic, and unsettling atmosphere of loneliness and masterfully integrate it into complex, dynamic dance rhythms. It is no longer merely a therapeutic diary within four walls, but an emergence into the level of mature, complex sound design.

Three global changes in sound that vqopb introduced:

1.    *Synthesis of isolation and large-scale groove. * While I AM ABSOLUTELY SURE... was locked-in, quiet, and domestic, vqopb proves that paranoia or deep melancholy can sound large, groovy, and professional — crossing unsettling weirdcore with old-school drum and bass and acid techno.

2.    *Cinematic quality at a new level. * Earlier, cinematic quality was built on household sounds (whispers, pills). In vqopb, it becomes epic and sci-fi. "arsenal gear ambient" is a full-blown, Hollywood-level soundtrack to a spy techno-thriller.

3.    *Mature deconstruction instead of chaotic noise. * On early releases, overload was often chaotic. Here, every glitch, every distortion loop, and every broken breakbeat line is calibrated with mathematical precision. It is "intelligent" aggression — wild, raw, and underground, but technically far superior to everything that came before autumn 2023, while retaining the same clipped style.
