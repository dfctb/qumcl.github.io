# Sound of Industrial Fallout

The sound of Industrial Fallout can be characterized as a viscous, post-apocalyptic psychedelic ambient with a deep immersion into the aesthetic of abandonment. Three key aspects define its audio landscape:

*Industrial atmosphere of decay. * True to its title, the music sounds like a soundtrack for wandering through empty factory halls, deserted railways, or endless Backrooms-style corridors. It is a dense, dark, and enveloping sound.

*Sluggish psychedelic experiment. * The compositions are long (averaging 8 to 11 minutes), allowing them to develop slowly and create a hypnotic, almost trance-like effect. Tracks such as "The Arrival Of Bad Trip" and "The Synthesize Experience" explore themes of altered consciousness and depressive trips through sonic distortion.

*Raw lo-fi and analog noise. * The sound consciously lacks the "polish" of commercial electronics. Elements of IDM, tape hiss, and lo-fi textures make the music tangible, "dirty", and alive. The artist has noted that this was his first experience with such a strange, unusual sound, inspired by an atmosphere of fear, darkness and loneliness.

Alongside music, Gerasenko continued to record various videos, ranging from memes to anime music videos (AMVs), and began making edit videos that were popular at the time.
